## Article 7 Storage and Security

### 7.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention | Basis |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Basic account information | 180 days after cancellation | Service contract |
| Transaction records | 5 years | E-Commerce Law, Art. 31 |
| Log data | 6 months | Cybersecurity Law, Art. 21 |
| AI-generated content | 30 days after user deletion or account cancellation | Service contract |
| Marketing-consent records | 3 years after consent withdrawal | GDPR compliance |

### 7.2 Security Measures

- **Transport encryption**: TLS 1.3 throughout
- **Storage encryption**: AES-256 at rest
- **Access control**: least-privilege principle + multi-factor authentication
- **Security auditing**: regular penetration testing and vulnerability scanning
- **Staff training**: regular data-security awareness training

### 7.3 Breach Notification

In the event of a data-security incident, we will:
- Report to the relevant regulator **within 72 hours** (GDPR requirement)
- **Promptly** notify affected users via platform announcement or email
- Take necessary remedial measures and provide ongoing updates

## Article 9 Your Rights

### 9.1 China Users (PIPL)

- **Right to know and decide**: understand and decide how your information is processed
- **Right to access and copy**: obtain a copy of your personal information
- **Right to rectify**: request correction of inaccurate information
- **Right to delete**: request deletion under statutory circumstances
- **Right to withdraw consent**: withdraw consent-based processing at any time
- **Right to portability**: request transfer of your information to a designated party

### 9.2 EU Users (GDPR)

- **Right of access** (Art. 15): obtain a copy of processed information
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16): correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17): request to be "forgotten"
- **Right to restriction** (Art. 18): restrict specific processing
- **Right to portability** (Art. 20): obtain data in a structured format
- **Right to object** (Art. 21): object to legitimate-interest processing
- **Rights re automated decision-making** (Art. 22): not be subject to purely automated decisions
- **Right to complain**: lodge a complaint with your member-state regulator

### 9.3 California Users (CCPA/CPRA)

- **Right to know**: the categories and purposes of collected information
- **Right to delete**: request deletion of personal information
- **Right to correct**: request correction of inaccurate information
- **Right to opt out of sale/sharing**: via the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link
- **Right to limit sensitive-information use**: restrict use of sensitive personal information
- **Right to non-discrimination**: no discriminatory treatment for exercising privacy rights

### 9.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit a request via:
- **Email**: privacy@tuiliangniu.com
- **In-platform**: Account Settings → Privacy & Security → Data Requests
- **Response time**: within 30 days of receipt (GDPR: 1 month; CCPA: 45 days)
